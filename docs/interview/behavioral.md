## Story templates
1. **Hardest technical problem** — what blocked you, what hypotheses you tested, how you proved the root cause.
2. **Failure / mistake** — what went wrong, impact, what process changed.
3. **Leadership / ownership without authority.**
4. **Conflict with collaborator / advisor.**
5. **Ambiguity — you had to define the problem.**
6. **Fast learning — picked up new stack (e.g. Isaac Lab) in X weeks.**
7. **Tradeoff decision — accuracy vs latency, build vs buy.**
8. **Mentoring / teaching.**
9. **Safety / ethics concern you raised.**
10. **Biggest impact you had.**

## Research-style questions
- Describe your thesis in 2 minutes (problem → gap → approach → result → impact).
- What are the limitations of your work? What would you do next?
- How does your survey work position you to pick promising directions? (taxonomy, gaps, open problems)
- How do you read a paper fast? (abstract → figures → method → experiments → limitations).
- How do you validate a result (ablations, seeds, baselines, statistical tests)?

## Questions to ask them
Team's stack and sim-to-real strategy; data collection scale; evaluation methodology; hardware iteration speed; on-call/ deployment cadence; how they prioritize research vs product; growth path.

## Tips
Quantify (latency ↓ 40 %, success 72→94 %), own the “I”, admit uncertainty, tie back to the role’s needs.
