### L1 regularisation (Lasso)

**ML** · Penalises the sum of absolute weights; drives some weights to exactly zero (sparsity).

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### L2 regularisation (Ridge, weight decay)

**ML** · Penalises the sum of squared weights; shrinks weights smoothly.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md) · [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Label

**ML** · The correct answer attached to a training example.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Label smoothing

**DL** · Replacing hard one-hot targets with softened ones to reduce over-confidence.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Lagrange multiplier

**Math** · A variable that turns a constrained optimisation problem into an unconstrained one by pricing the constraint.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md)

### Lagrangian mechanics

**Robotics** · Deriving equations of motion from kinetic minus potential energy.

*Read more:* [Dynamics](../08-robotics/dynamics.md)

### Laplace transform

**Math** · Converts differential equations in time into algebra in the variable s; the basis of transfer functions in classical control.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md) · [Control](../10-planning-control/control.md)

### Latency

**CS** · The delay between a cause and its effect (e.g. camera frame to motor command).

*Read more:* [CS & algorithms](../01-foundations/cs-dsa-complexity.md) · [Control](../10-planning-control/control.md)

### Launch file

**Software** · A script that starts and configures many ROS nodes together.

*Read more:* [ROS 2](../12-software/ros2.md)

### Law of large numbers

**Math** · Sample averages converge to the true expectation as the number of samples grows.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md)

### Layer normalisation

**DL** · Normalising across features within each example; standard in transformers.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md) · [DL architectures](../03-deep-learning/architectures.md)

### LEAP Hand

**Platform** · A low-cost 16-DoF dexterous hand designed for research.

*Read more:* [Robots & platforms](../reference/robots-platforms.md) · [Manipulation & dexterity](../11-mobility-dexterity/manipulation-dexterity.md)

### Learning curve

**ML** · A plot of training and validation error against data size or training time; diagnoses bias and variance.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Learning rate

**DL** · The step size of an optimiser; the most important hyper-parameter.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Learning-rate warm-up

**DL** · Starting with a tiny learning rate and ramping it up, to avoid early instability.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Learning to rank

**ML** · Training models to order items by relevance.

*Read more:* [More ML topics](../02-machine-learning/ml-more-topics.md)

### Least squares

**Math** · Choosing parameters that minimise the sum of squared errors; solved by the normal equations or QR/SVD.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md) · [SLAM & localization](../09-perception-localization/slam-localization.md)

### legged_gym

**Sim** · ETH Zürich's Isaac Gym-based framework for learning legged locomotion.

*Read more:* [Isaac Gym](../13-simulation/isaac-gym.md) · [Legged locomotion](../11-mobility-dexterity/legged-locomotion.md)

### Legged robot

**Robotics** · A robot that moves by stepping (biped, quadruped, hexapod).

*Read more:* [Legged locomotion](../11-mobility-dexterity/legged-locomotion.md)

### LeRobot

**VLA** · Hugging Face's library for robot datasets, policies (ACT, Diffusion, SmolVLA, π0) and low-cost hardware.

*Read more:* [Build a VLA](../build-your-own/03-build-a-vla.md)

### LIBERO

**Sim** · A benchmark of language-conditioned manipulation tasks used to compare VLAs.

*Read more:* [Build a VLA](../build-your-own/03-build-a-vla.md)

### LiDAR

**Perception** · Light detection and ranging: measures distance with laser pulses to build 3-D point clouds.

*Read more:* [Sensors & actuators](../08-robotics/sensors-actuators.md) · [SLAM & localization](../09-perception-localization/slam-localization.md)

### Lie algebra

**Math** · The tangent space of a Lie group at the identity (small rotations as vectors).

*Read more:* [More math: numerics & graphical models](../01-foundations/more-math-numerics-graphical-models.md) · [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Lie group

**Math** · A smooth group such as SO(3) or SE(3); optimisation on poses uses its exponential and logarithm maps.

*Read more:* [More math: numerics & graphical models](../01-foundations/more-math-numerics-graphical-models.md) · [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Lifecycle node

**Software** · A managed ROS 2 node with defined states (unconfigured, inactive, active, finalised).

*Read more:* [ROS 2](../12-software/ros2.md)

### LightGBM

**ML** · A gradient-boosting library using histogram splits and leaf-wise growth for speed.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Li-ion / LiFePO₄ battery

**Hardware** · Rechargeable lithium battery chemistries; LiFePO₄ is safer and longer-lived, Li-ion has higher energy density.

*Read more:* [Mechanics & electronics](../08-robotics/mechanics-electronics.md)

### Likelihood

**Math** · How probable the observed data is under a given model parameter; a function of the parameter, not a distribution over it.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md)

### Linear inverted pendulum model (LIPM)

**Robotics** · A simple walking model: a point mass at constant height on a massless leg.

*Read more:* [Dynamics](../08-robotics/dynamics.md) · [Legged locomotion](../11-mobility-dexterity/legged-locomotion.md)

### Linear regression

**ML** · Fitting a straight line (or plane) to predict a number by minimising squared error.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Link

**Robotics** · A rigid body between two joints.

*Read more:* [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### LIO-SAM

**Perception** · A tightly coupled LiDAR-inertial odometry method using a factor graph with GNSS and loop closures.

*Read more:* [SLAM & localization](../09-perception-localization/slam-localization.md)

### LLaVA

**VLA** · A VLM recipe: CLIP vision encoder + MLP connector + LLM, trained in two stages.

*Read more:* [VLM & VLA](../05-foundation-models/vlm-vla.md) · [Build a VLM](../build-your-own/02-build-a-vlm.md)

### LLM

**LLM** · Large language model: a very large transformer trained to predict the next token.

*Read more:* [LLMs](../05-foundation-models/llm.md)

### LLM-as-judge

**LLM** · Using a strong model to grade other models' outputs; prone to length and position bias.

*Read more:* [LLMs](../05-foundation-models/llm.md) · [Evaluate, deploy, debug](../build-your-own/04-evaluate-deploy-debug.md)

### Localisation

**Perception** · Estimating the robot's pose in a known map.

*Read more:* [SLAM & localization](../09-perception-localization/slam-localization.md)

### Lock-free queue

**Software** · A concurrent queue that avoids mutexes so a real-time thread never blocks.

*Read more:* [C++](../12-software/cpp-for-robotics.md)

### Loco-manipulation

**Robotics** · Combining locomotion and manipulation, e.g. a humanoid walking while carrying an object.

### Logical qubit

### Logistic regression

**ML** · A linear classifier that outputs probabilities via the sigmoid function.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Log-likelihood

**Math** · The logarithm of the likelihood; turns products into sums and is what training losses usually minimise (negated).

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md) · [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Loop closure

**Perception** · Recognising a previously visited place and adding a constraint that corrects accumulated drift.

*Read more:* [SLAM & localization](../09-perception-localization/slam-localization.md)

### LoRA

**DL** · Low-rank adaptation: fine-tune a model by training small low-rank matrices added to frozen weights.

### Loss function

**ML** · The number a model tries to minimise during training, measuring how wrong predictions are.

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md) · [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Loss landscape

**DL** · The shape of the loss as a function of parameters; training is walking downhill on it.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### LQG

**Control** · LQR combined with a Kalman filter.

*Read more:* [Control](../10-planning-control/control.md) · [State estimation](../09-perception-localization/state-estimation.md)

### LQR

**Control** · Linear-quadratic regulator: optimal state feedback for linear systems with quadratic cost.

*Read more:* [Control](../10-planning-control/control.md)

### LSTM

**DL** · Long short-term memory: an RNN with gates and a cell state that carries information over long spans.

*Read more:* [DL architectures](../03-deep-learning/architectures.md)

### LU decomposition

**Math** · Factoring a matrix into lower and upper triangular matrices for solving linear systems.

*Read more:* [More math: numerics & graphical models](../01-foundations/more-math-numerics-graphical-models.md)
