### Jacobian (math)

**Math** · The matrix of first partial derivatives of a vector-valued function; the local linear approximation. See also the robot Jacobian.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md) · [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Jacobian (robot)

**Robotics** · The matrix mapping joint velocities to end-effector velocity; also maps end-effector force to joint torque.

*Read more:* [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### JAX

**Software** · A numerical library with autodiff, JIT compilation and vectorisation (vmap) for accelerators.

*Read more:* [Python](../12-software/python-for-robotics.md) · [Simulation landscape](../13-simulation/simulation-landscape.md)

### Jensen's inequality

**Math** · For a convex function f, f(average of x) is at most the average of f(x); the basis of the ELBO in variational inference.

*Read more:* [Math foundations](../01-foundations/linear-algebra-probability.md)

### JEPA

**DL** · Joint-embedding predictive architecture: predicts the representation of missing parts instead of their pixels.

*Read more:* [DL architectures](../03-deep-learning/architectures.md)

### Jetson

**Software** · NVIDIA's family of embedded GPU modules (Orin, Thor) for robots.

*Read more:* [Dev tools](../12-software/devtools-deployment.md) · [Physical AI](../07-physical-ai/physical-ai-overview.md)

### Jitter

**CS** · Variation in latency from one cycle to the next; the enemy of real-time control.

*Read more:* [CS & algorithms](../01-foundations/cs-dsa-complexity.md) · [C++](../12-software/cpp-for-robotics.md)

### Joint

**Robotics** · A connection that allows relative motion between links: revolute (rotary) or prismatic (sliding).

*Read more:* [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Joint space

**Robotics** · The space of joint angles or positions.

*Read more:* [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Jordan–Wigner transformation

**Quantum** · A mapping from fermionic operators (chemistry) to qubit operators.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)
