### QAOA

**Quantum** · Quantum approximate optimisation algorithm: alternates problem and mixer layers with tuned angles to solve combinatorial problems.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Q-Former

**VLA** · A small transformer with learnable queries that compresses vision features into a fixed number of tokens (BLIP-2).

*Read more:* [VLM & VLA](../05-foundation-models/vlm-vla.md)

### Q-function

**RL** · The expected return of taking an action in a state and following the policy afterwards.

*Read more:* [RL foundations](../04-reinforcement-learning/rl-foundations.md)

### Qiskit

**Quantum** · IBM's open-source Python framework for quantum circuits, transpilation and execution.

*Read more:* [Quantum software](../06-quantum/quantum-software.md)

### Q-learning

**RL** · An off-policy method that learns Q-values using the maximum over next actions.

*Read more:* [RL foundations](../04-reinforcement-learning/rl-foundations.md)

### QLoRA

**LLM** · LoRA fine-tuning on top of a 4-bit quantised base model to save memory.

*Read more:* [LLMs](../05-foundation-models/llm.md) · [Build an AI model](../build-your-own/01-build-an-ai-model.md)

### QoS (quality of service)

**Software** · DDS delivery policies in ROS 2: reliability, durability, history, deadline.

*Read more:* [ROS 2](../12-software/ros2.md)

### QR decomposition

**Math** · Factoring a matrix into an orthogonal and an upper-triangular matrix; used for least squares.

*Read more:* [More math: numerics & graphical models](../01-foundations/more-math-numerics-graphical-models.md)

### Quadrotor

**Mobility** · A drone with four rotors; underactuated, differentially flat.

*Read more:* [Autonomous driving & drones](../11-mobility-dexterity/autonomous-driving-uav.md)

### Quadruped

**Robotics** · A four-legged robot (Spot, ANYmal, Unitree Go2).

*Read more:* [Legged locomotion](../11-mobility-dexterity/legged-locomotion.md)

### Quantisation

**LLM** · Storing weights or activations in fewer bits (8, 4) to cut memory and latency.

*Read more:* [LLMs](../05-foundation-models/llm.md) · [Dev tools](../12-software/devtools-deployment.md)

### Quantum advantage

**Quantum** · Solving a task faster or better than any known classical method.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum annealer

**Quantum** · A device (D-Wave) that performs quantum annealing for optimisation problems.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum circuit

**Quantum** · A sequence of gates and measurements acting on qubits.

*Read more:* [Quantum basics](../06-quantum/quantum-basics.md) · [Quantum software](../06-quantum/quantum-software.md)

### Quantum Fourier transform (QFT)

**Quantum** · The quantum version of the discrete Fourier transform; a core subroutine of Shor's algorithm and phase estimation.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum kernel

**Quantum** · A similarity function computed as the overlap of quantum feature states; used with classical SVMs.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum key distribution (QKD)

**Quantum** · Sharing cryptographic keys with security guaranteed by quantum mechanics (BB84).

*Read more:* [Quantum basics](../06-quantum/quantum-basics.md)

### Quantum machine learning (QML)

**Quantum** · Machine learning that uses quantum circuits as models or kernels.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum phase estimation (QPE)

**Quantum** · Estimates the eigenvalue (phase) of a unitary operator; underlies Shor and chemistry algorithms.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum sensing

**Quantum** · Using quantum effects (NV centres, atom interferometry, SQUIDs) to measure fields, time and motion with high precision.

*Read more:* [Sensors & actuators](../08-robotics/sensors-actuators.md) · [Space & other robots](../11-mobility-dexterity/space-humanoid-other.md)

### Quantum supremacy

**Quantum** · Demonstrating a quantum device performing a task infeasible for classical computers, regardless of usefulness.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quantum walk

**Quantum** · The quantum analogue of a random walk, used in search and graph algorithms.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)

### Quasi-direct drive (QDD)

**Hardware** · An actuator with a low gear ratio (about 6 to 10) giving backdrivability and fast torque response.

### Quasi-Newton (BFGS)

**Math** · Optimisation methods that approximate the Hessian from gradient history.

*Read more:* [More math: numerics & graphical models](../01-foundations/more-math-numerics-graphical-models.md)

### Quaternion

**Robotics** · Four-number unit representation of rotation without gimbal lock.

*Read more:* [Kinematics](../08-robotics/kinematics.md)

### Qubit

**Quantum** · The basic unit of quantum information: a two-level system in a superposition of 0 and 1.

*Read more:* [Quantum basics](../06-quantum/quantum-basics.md)

### QUBO

**Quantum** · Quadratic unconstrained binary optimisation: minimise x-transpose Q x over bits; the native format of annealers.

*Read more:* [Quantum algorithms](../06-quantum/quantum-algorithms.md)
