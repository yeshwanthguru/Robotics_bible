### Backdrivability

**Perception** · Detecting moving objects by comparing frames to a background model.

### Backlash

**Robotics** · Play in a gear train that causes lost motion when direction reverses.

*Read more:* [Sensors & actuators](../08-robotics/sensors-actuators.md)

### Backpropagation

**DL** · Computing gradients of the loss with respect to every weight by applying the chain rule backwards through the network.

*Read more:* [DL fundamentals](../03-deep-learning/dl-fundamentals.md)

### Bagging

**ML** · Training many models on random resamples of the data and averaging them to reduce variance (random forest).

*Read more:* [ML fundamentals](../02-machine-learning/ml-fundamentals.md)

### Bag of words

**NLP** · Representing text as word counts, ignoring order.

### Barren plateau

**Quantum** · A region of a variational cost landscape where gradients vanish exponentially, making training impossible.

### Batch (mini-batch)

**ML** · A small group of examples processed together in one training step.

**DL** · Normalising a layer's inputs across the batch to stabilise and speed up training; differs between training and test.

**Math** · Updating a probability distribution over unknown quantities using Bayes' rule as data arrives.

**Math** · A directed graph of random variables with conditional probability tables; encodes dependencies compactly.

**ML** · Optimising an expensive function using a probabilistic surrogate (Gaussian process) to choose where to evaluate next.

**Math** · p(a given b) = p(b given a) × p(a) / p(b): how to update a belief when new evidence arrives. The root of every filter in robotics.
