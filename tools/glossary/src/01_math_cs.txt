Argmax / argmin | Math | The input value at which a function reaches its maximum (argmax) or minimum (argmin), as opposed to the maximum value itself. | math
Basis (linear algebra) | Math | A set of linearly independent vectors that can build every vector in a space by weighted sums; the number of vectors is the dimension. | math
Bayes' rule | Math | p(a given b) = p(b given a) × p(a) / p(b): how to update a belief when new evidence arrives. The root of every filter in robotics. | math,est
Bayesian inference | Math | Updating a probability distribution over unknown quantities using Bayes' rule as data arrives. | math
Bias (statistical) | Math | The systematic difference between an estimator's average value and the true value. Different from the bias term in a neural network. | math,ml
Binomial distribution | Math | The number of successes in n independent yes/no trials, each with success probability p. Used for success-rate statistics. | math
Central limit theorem | Math | The average of many independent random samples is approximately Gaussian, whatever the original distribution. | math
Chain rule | Math | The derivative of a composed function is the product of the derivatives of its parts; the mathematical basis of backpropagation. | math,dl
Cholesky decomposition | Math | Factoring a symmetric positive-definite matrix A as L times L-transpose with L lower-triangular; used for fast solves and sampling Gaussians. | math
Condition number | Math | The ratio of largest to smallest singular value of a matrix; large values mean the problem is nearly singular and noise gets amplified. | math,kin
Conditional probability | Math | The probability of an event given that another event has occurred, written p(a given b). | math
Confidence interval | Math | A range computed from data that would contain the true value a stated fraction (e.g. 95 percent) of the time over repeated experiments. | math,evaldeploy
Convex function | Math | A function whose line between any two points lies above its graph; every local minimum is a global minimum. | math
Convex optimization | Math | Minimising a convex function over a convex set; reliably solvable. Includes LP, QP, SOCP and SDP. | math
Correlation | Math | A normalised measure (between −1 and 1) of how linearly two variables move together. | math
Covariance | Math | How two variables vary together; the covariance matrix describes the shape of an uncertainty cloud. | math,est
Cross product | Math | A 3-D vector perpendicular to two input vectors with length equal to the area they span; appears in torque and angular velocity. | math,kin
Determinant | Math | A single number from a square matrix: the factor by which it scales volume; zero means the matrix is singular. | math
Dot product | Math | The sum of products of matching entries of two vectors; measures alignment and projects one vector onto another. | math
Eigenvalue and eigenvector | Math | A vector that a matrix only stretches (not turns) and the stretch factor. Eigenvalues determine stability and principal directions. | math
Entropy | Math | The average surprise of a random variable, H = −sum p log p; measures uncertainty. | math
Euclidean distance | Math | The straight-line distance between two points, the square root of the sum of squared coordinate differences. | math
Expectation | Math | The probability-weighted average value of a random variable. | math
Fourier transform | Math | Rewrites a signal as a sum of sine waves of different frequencies; used for filtering, vibration analysis and the quantum Fourier transform's classical cousin. | math
Gaussian distribution | Math | The bell curve defined by a mean and a variance (or covariance matrix); closed under linear maps, which makes Kalman filters exact. | math,est
Gradient | Math | The vector of partial derivatives of a function; points in the direction of steepest increase. | math,dl
Hessian | Math | The matrix of second derivatives of a function; describes curvature. | math
Identity matrix | Math | The square matrix with ones on the diagonal; multiplying by it changes nothing. | math
Jacobian (math) | Math | The matrix of first partial derivatives of a vector-valued function; the local linear approximation. See also the robot Jacobian. | math,kin
Jensen's inequality | Math | For a convex function f, f(average of x) is at most the average of f(x); the basis of the ELBO in variational inference. | math
KL divergence | Math | A non-symmetric measure of how one probability distribution differs from another; zero only when they are equal. | math,dl
Lagrange multiplier | Math | A variable that turns a constrained optimisation problem into an unconstrained one by pricing the constraint. | math
Law of large numbers | Math | Sample averages converge to the true expectation as the number of samples grows. | math
Least squares | Math | Choosing parameters that minimise the sum of squared errors; solved by the normal equations or QR/SVD. | math,slam
Likelihood | Math | How probable the observed data is under a given model parameter; a function of the parameter, not a distribution over it. | math
Log-likelihood | Math | The logarithm of the likelihood; turns products into sums and is what training losses usually minimise (negated). | math,dl
Mahalanobis distance | Math | Distance measured in units of standard deviation that accounts for correlations; used to gate outliers in filters. | math,est
Markov chain | Math | A random process where the next state depends only on the current state. | math,rl
Markov property | Math | The future is independent of the past given the present state. | math,rl
Matrix inverse | Math | The matrix that undoes another matrix; exists only for square, non-singular matrices. | math
Maximum a posteriori (MAP) | Math | The parameter value with the highest posterior probability; equals maximum likelihood plus a prior, which acts as regularisation. | math,ml
Maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) | Math | Choosing the parameters that make the observed data most probable. | math,ml
Monte Carlo method | Math | Estimating quantities by random sampling; error shrinks as one over the square root of the sample count. | math,est
Mutual information | Math | How much knowing one variable reduces uncertainty about another. | math
Norm | Math | A measure of vector size: L1 (sum of absolute values), L2 (Euclidean length), infinity-norm (largest entry). | math
Null space | Math | The set of inputs a matrix maps to zero; in redundant robot arms it is the motion that does not move the hand. | math,kin

P-value | Math | The probability of seeing data at least this extreme if the null hypothesis were true; not the probability the hypothesis is true. | math,r
Poisson distribution | Math | The number of events in a fixed time when events occur independently at a constant average rate. | math
Positive definite matrix | Math | A symmetric matrix with all positive eigenvalues; defines a bowl-shaped energy. Covariances and inertia matrices are positive (semi-)definite. | math,dyn
Posterior | Math | The updated belief about a quantity after seeing the data. | math
Prior | Math | The belief about a quantity before seeing the data. | math
Probability density function (PDF) | Math | A function whose area over an interval gives the probability that a continuous variable falls in it. | math

Rank | Math | The number of linearly independent rows or columns of a matrix; the number of directions it can really act in. | math,kin

Taylor series | Math | Approximating a function near a point by a polynomial built from its derivatives; the basis of linearisation in the EKF. | math,est
Trace | Math | The sum of a square matrix's diagonal entries; equals the sum of its eigenvalues. | math
Transpose | Math | Flipping a matrix across its diagonal, turning rows into columns. | math
Variance | Math | The average squared distance from the mean; measures spread. | math
