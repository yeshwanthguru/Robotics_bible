Error correction (quantum) | Quantum | Encoding logical qubits across many physical qubits to detect and fix errors (surface code). | qbasics
Error mitigation | Quantum | Post-processing techniques that reduce noise effects on near-term devices without full error correction. | qbasics
Fault tolerance | Quantum | Computing reliably with noisy components by using error correction below a threshold error rate. | qbasics
Fidelity | Quantum | A measure (0 to 1) of how close two quantum states or operations are. | qbasics
Gate (quantum) | Quantum | A reversible (unitary) operation on one or more qubits. | qbasics

Logical qubit | Quantum | An error-protected qubit encoded in many physical qubits. | qbasics

NISQ | Quantum | Noisy intermediate-scale quantum: today's devices with tens to thousands of noisy qubits. | qbasics

Quantum advantage | Quantum | Solving a task faster or better than any known classical method. | qalgo
Quantum annealer | Quantum | A device (D-Wave) that performs quantum annealing for optimisation problems. | qalgo

Quantum key distribution (QKD) | Quantum | Sharing cryptographic keys with security guaranteed by quantum mechanics (BB84). | qbasics
Quantum machine learning (QML) | Quantum | Machine learning that uses quantum circuits as models or kernels. | qalgo
